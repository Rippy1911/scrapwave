## Look and world pass (lane A, 2026-11)

The textures and models added above were fetched from Poly Haven at 1k, re-encoded (JPEG q82-85, mipmapped in the texture arrays), and the meshes simplified with meshoptimizer (1.4k-6k triangles), rescaled to unit size and re-centred; the rock models carry no textures of their own (one shared strata shader). Total added: 3.9 MB.

- `../favicon.ico` (in `public/`): original, drawn for Scrapwake (a mesa on a dark ground). CC0.
- Grass tufts, tents, tarps, ruins and power lines are procedural geometry in `src/game/world/`, no files.

- 2026-11-10 asset diet: the larger models were welded, de-duplicated and simplified (bounding boxes unchanged within 0.3%), their embedded textures resized to 512-768 px and re-encoded as WebP, the `textures/` JPGs re-encoded at lower quality (the metal sets at 512 px), and `hdri/quarry_01_1k.hdr` was box-filtered down to 512x256. File names, paths and licences are unchanged.
